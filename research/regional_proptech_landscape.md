# Regional PropTech / ConTech landscape relevant to JCX

**Research cut-off:** 30 August 2026 (Asia/Dhaka)  
**Scope:** Bangladesh first; India/South Asia; MENA (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt); Southeast Asia; Japan; Australia. “PropTech” is used broadly for the property lifecycle (land/data, development, construction, sales, financing, operations and resident experience). “ConTech” covers construction procurement, site control, BIM/reality capture and facilities operations.

## Executive readout

The highest-value lesson for a Bangladeshi developer is not to copy a property portal. The best regional companies own a painful workflow and compound proprietary data: (1) India’s developer revenue operating systems (Sell.Do), (2) construction procurement plus credit (Infra.Market, BRKZ), (3) site execution and worker/equipment evidence (WakeCap, Powerplay), (4) property operations and energy data (Facilio, Zenatix), (5) title/feasibility data (Landeed, Archistar), and (6) regulated transaction/financing rails (Nawy, Huspy, SmartCrowd/PRYPCO).

For JCX, a practical architecture is a **developer operating system** rather than a consumer marketplace: a Bengali/English project, land/JV, unit-inventory, collections, procurement, site-progress, handover and investor/customer portal, with an auditable event/data layer. A marketplace can be a distribution channel (Bproperty, PropertyLand), but listing volume without verified inventory, title evidence and conversion/collection data is weak defensibility.

Bangladesh has unusually strong whitespace in five linked layers: **title and feasibility intelligence; developer-native ERP; construction procurement with transparent quotations and delivery; shareholder/JV governance; and post-handover operations/energy**. Existing government systems (e-Mutation, e-Porcha, land-tax and planned e-registration connections) make integration strategically more valuable, but they do not eliminate the need for professional legal/title review.

The immediate shortlist for JCX diligence is:

| Tier | Companies / ecosystems | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| A — pilot/partner now | PropERP, Vextrus, E-Hishabi, BuildMart, NirmanBazaar, Barikoi; Sell.Do; Powerplay; Facilio/Zenatix; Archistar (concept benchmark) | Local workflow fit or proven category play; test on one project with measurable baseline. |
| B — strategic benchmark/partnership | PropertyLand, ShareBari, Truzo, Jomee Jomaa; Landeed, Infra.Market; BRKZ, WakeCap; Nawy, Huspy; PropertyGuru/99 Group; GA/RENOSY | Strong product or business-model lessons; cross-border licensing requires localization. |
| C — watch/caution | Bproperty (ownership/domain discontinuity); NoBroker (scale and monetization tension); Homebase (name collision and acquired US product); tokenized/fractional models generally | Useful patterns, but verify legal status, customer outcomes, liquidity and data rights before reliance. |

Evidence grades used here: **A** = regulator, exchange filing, government program, or dated company release with a concrete event; **B** = company product page/official ecosystem profile with live operating evidence but no independent outcome audit; **C** = reputable press, accelerator/VC profile or app-store evidence; **D** = only a self-claim, social profile or uncorroborated directory. Funding is recorded only when dated and source-backed; “customers,” “GMV,” “ROI” and “No.1” are company claims unless independently corroborated.

## 1. Bangladesh: market, infrastructure and constraints

### Structural context

REHAB’s public directory contained **924 members** when checked (August 2026), illustrating a fragmented developer base rather than a single consolidated buyer. The 2024 REHAB Fair reported **Tk403.13 crore** of sales/bookings and more than 17,000 visitors, a useful signal of market activity but not a total-market estimate. DBH’s 2023 annual report cites roughly 15,000 flats sold nationally per year (a sector estimate, not a transaction registry). Sources: [REHAB member directory](https://www.rehab-bd.org/rehab-member/), [TBS report on REHAB Fair 2024](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/sales-bookings-flats-plots-spaces-exceed-tk403cr-rehab-fair-1028536), [DBH annual report 2023](https://dbhfinance.com/public/assets/investor/financial-statement-sub/1714286608_DBH_Annual_Report_2023.pdf).

Land records remain a multi-agency, document-heavy risk. The Ministry of Land’s online mutation guidance supports online applications, document upload and BDT70 online fees through cards/mobile financial services ([e-Mutation guidance](https://mutation.land.gov.bd/nirdeshika)). The government’s 8th Five Year Plan describes digital registration and district land-registry expansion, while a budget speech described linking e-registration, Digital Record Room and e-Mutation so registration can trigger mutation automatically ([8th Five Year Plan](https://macro.finance.gov.bd/Files/PoliciesGuidelines/8th%20Five%20Year%20Plan.pdf), [budget speech / e-registration linkage](https://www.bssnews.net/assets/gallery/Finance_Minister_Budget_Speech2022_23.pdf)). These are enabling infrastructure and policy direction, not a guarantee that any third-party startup has API access or that a title is clean.

Operating realities specific to JCX include joint-venture landowner/developer allocations; long BDT installment cycles and post-dated cheques; hand-entered site registers and running-account bills; informal broker/channel-partner networks; Bengali/English and Banglish communications; mobile-money collection; Dhaka/Chattogram geography and monsoon/logistics variability; and a need to serve NRBs without exposing NID/title data. Any product imported from India or the Gulf must be adapted to Bangladesh deeds, khatian/dag/mouza concepts, mutation, RAJUK/CDA approvals, VAT/Mushak, TDS/VDS, BDT/lakh/crore and local payment rails.

### Bangladesh companies — verified and downgraded

#### Bproperty — formerly a major portal, but treat current continuity as unresolved

**Category:** marketplace/transaction services; **entity:** private portal. Bproperty historically received a reported **US$10m EMPG investment in 2019** ([Financial Express/BSS](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/public/print/10m-investment-for-real-estate-dev-1551026076)); its parent EMPG was reported at a **US$150m investment / US$1bn valuation in 2020**, with Bproperty among its portals ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/business/real-estate/208904/bproperty%E2%80%99s-parent-empg-raises-150m-in)). BSS reported a January 2023 combination with Australian Digital Classifieds Group ([BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/business/107809)).

The current domain itself now says it is **under new ownership, not affiliated with Bproperty.com Limited**, and was purchased as a standalone asset from DCG ([current about page](https://www.bproperty.com/about-us), [terms](https://www.bproperty.com/terms-of-use)). This is a material diligence issue: a live listing site is not proof that the legacy operating company, seller contracts, historical inventory or data rights transferred. **Grade A for historical events; Grade B/D for current operating continuity.**

**JCX use:** benchmark listing UX and lead generation only after verifying entity, contracts, data ownership, listing freshness, lead-to-booking attribution and any Rupayan/other ownership assertions. Do not base market-size claims on the new site’s self-description.

#### PropertyLand — live, but traction is self-reported

Launched in 2024, PropertyLand describes a marketplace combined with legal guidance, investment and consultancy. Its page reports **300+ clients, 1,000+ properties, 70+ brands and 500 daily visitors**, all company claims ([About / metrics](https://www.propertyland.co/footer/about)). **Grade B for product existence; D for unverified traction.**

**JCX use:** benchmark the “listing + legal workflow” bundle; possible secondary-market/partner channel. Require lawyer-reviewed title process, SLA, actual conversion and data-processing terms before integration.

#### Barikoi — high-signal geospatial infrastructure, not a property marketplace

Founded in Bangladesh (reported 2017/2018), Barikoi provides local maps, location APIs and location data for logistics, retail, financial institutions and government. Crunchbase records an active Dhaka company and Iterative/AcceliCITY relationships ([Crunchbase profile](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/barikoi)); IDLC Venture Capital publicly announced an equity investment in 2024 and described its mapping infrastructure and enterprise customers ([IDLC announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/idlc-asset-management-limited_idlc-venture-capitalhas-made-an-equity-investment-activity-7196948544384098304-EgcV)). An AsiaBerlin 2025 profile said it had raised over US$500k and was expanding toward Singapore/MENA ([AsiaBerlin profile](https://abs2025.asia.berlin/opportunities/UGFydGljaXBhdGlvbjE5Nzk0MA%3D%3D)).

**JCX use:** geocode projects, map service catchments/drive times, normalize addresses, support site selection and delivery routing. Test positional accuracy, update frequency, offline operation, API uptime, licensing and whether sensitive title coordinates are retained. **Grade B/A for existence and investment; do not extrapolate property valuation accuracy.**

#### Vextrus — promising early-stage Bangladesh-native ERP

Vextrus positions itself as an AI-native ERP for Bangladesh construction and real estate: land/title, unit inventory, sales/booking, collections, handover, HR/payroll, procurement, projects, ledger and NBR tax. Its product page says agents draft but require named human approval and audit logs, and explicitly supports Bangla, BDT/lakh/crore, Mushak 6.3/6.5/9.1 and the July–June fiscal year ([Vextrus](https://www.vextrus.com/)). A LinkedIn profile identifies it as founded in 2026 with a Dhaka presence ([LinkedIn](https://bd.linkedin.com/company/vextrus)).

This is a **pilot candidate, not a proven enterprise deployment**: “24 AI agents,” quantified savings and founding-team claims are company claims. **Grade B for live product; D for outcome claims.** Pilot on one JCX project with data export, audit-log, segregation-of-duties, backup/restore, uptime and rollback requirements.

#### E-Hishabi — local real-estate ERP product

E-Hishabi says it is ERP specifically for Bangladesh developers/builders covering land, sales, collection, procurement, construction, HR and accounts in one database ([E-Hishabi](https://ehishabi.com/)). Public proof of customer count, audited outcomes, pricing and security posture was not found in this review. **Grade B for product existence; D for traction/outcomes.**

#### PropERP (SpaGreen Creative) — strongest local product signal for developer sales/collections

PropERP identifies SpaGreen Creative as its Dhaka builder and says the software company has been building SaaS/ERP/mobile apps since 2018. Its bilingual modules include flat booking, installment collection, land/plot projects, joint ventures, developer CRM, WhatsApp/SMS and broker commissions ([Bangla product page](https://properp.app/bn), [product page](https://properp.app/)). Its own page claims 8,200+ businesses served, 110+ countries and 350+ delivered projects—these figures appear to describe SpaGreen’s broader company activity, not necessarily PropERP customers, and should not be treated as independently verified.

**JCX use:** high-fit pilot for booking, kisti, JV, broker and NRB workflows. Compare against Vextrus/E-Hishabi on construction cost depth, API/data export, hosting, payment reconciliation and implementation ownership. **Grade B for product; D for scale metrics.**

#### Unova Estate — live local ERP/CRM, but aggressive metrics are claims

Unova markets an AI-powered ERP/CRM for Bangladeshi builders, land developers and sales agencies, with 11 modules (CRM, property/project, sales/commissions, land/feasibility, BOQ, procurement, contractors, construction progress, HR/payroll, finance and role-based access) ([Unova Estate](https://www.estate.unova.bd/), [developer solution](https://www.estate.unova.bd/solutions/erp-for-real-estate-developers)). It claims a 6-month refund guarantee, “45% efficiency,” 91% on-time collections, 75% material-wastage savings and zero double bookings, but no independent case-study audit was located. **Grade B for live product; D for outcomes.**

**JCX use:** benchmark local-language UX, commission/PDC workflows, JV split logic and mobile field use. Demand references, security documentation, data portability, liability boundaries for AI and a measurable baseline before accepting savings claims.

#### ProQ — procurement marketplace, very early evidence

ProQ’s LinkedIn profile describes a Dhaka construction/architecture/real-estate procurement marketplace, founded in 2025, with a 2–10 employee company size and a small follower base ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proq-platform)). The profile is useful for existence and positioning, but not customer traction or fulfillment capability. **Grade C/D; watchlist/pilot only.**

#### BuildMart — construction-material and services platform; validate fulfillment

BuildMart says it is an initiative of Buildcon Bangladesh and an integrated digital platform for materials and construction services, with supplier comparison, RFQs, verified professionals and local cost calculators ([About](https://buildmart.com.bd/pages/about-us)). The page describes a two-sided marketplace, not independently measured GMV, delivery performance or supplier quality. **Grade B for product existence; D for scale/outcomes.**

**JCX use:** run a controlled RFQ for cement/steel/finishing items. Track quote spread, authenticity, lead time, delivery completeness, credit terms, VAT invoice and dispute rate before moving critical-path procurement.

#### NirmanBazaar — more explicit procurement/credit proposition, still self-reported

NirmanBazaar describes a B2B/B2C materials platform with direct factory sourcing, project tracking/cost tools, bank-loan support, nationwide delivery, ERP/VAT-invoice integration and B2B credit. Its vendor page claims 1,000+ suppliers, 5,000+ buyers and 64+ districts; its main page claims 10,000+ verified products and up to 30–60 day credit ([main site](https://www.nirmanbazaar.com.bd/), [vendor page](https://info-vendor.nirmanbazaar.com/)). These are claims, not audited outcomes. **Grade B for a live proposition; D for volume/credit claims.**

**JCX use:** potentially stronger than a listing marketplace if it really controls quality, delivery and credit. Require factory authorization, product batch/quality testing, credit underwriting, working-capital exposure, return policy and proof that “ERP integration” supports JCX’s stack.

#### ShareBari — shareholder/JV governance niche

ShareBari is a Bangladesh-focused SaaS for apartment-share projects. It describes project setup, share/unit inventory, booking/land-registration/construction invoices, investor portals, document vaults, payment reminders and loyalty points; its live demo shows Padma Builders sample data ([ShareBari](https://sharebari.com/)). This is a compelling local workflow hypothesis, but the public page does not establish paying customer count or audited deployments. **Grade B for product; D for customer outcomes.**

**JCX use:** benchmark investor transparency and installment communications; pilot on a shared/JV project. Ensure shareholder privacy (no cross-investor leakage), legal relationship between a “share” and title/unit, invoice/tax treatment, exportability and committee approval controls.

#### Truzo — construction governance in pilot

Truzo explicitly labels itself **“Now in Pilot”** and describes proof-required expenses, committee approvals, shareholder statements, receipts and progress updates for shared apartment projects ([Truzo](https://www.truzo.app/)). It claims “zero disputes” and shows a Green Valley Heights example, but those are unverified company/customer claims. **Grade B for pilot existence; D for outcomes.**

**JCX use:** interesting narrow pilot where landowners/shareholders need evidence. It complements (rather than replaces) ERP/procurement: integrate approved expense IDs, BOQ, site photos, invoices and bank payment proofs. Check retention, tamper resistance and whether “proof” is actually independently validated.

#### Jomee Jomaa — fractional land investment; regulatory and liquidity caution

Jomee Jomaa launched publicly in October 2023 as a fractional land investment platform; Daily Star described BDT1,000 units, a 3–5 year appreciation model, secondary marketplace and 20% platform fee ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/fractional-land-investment-platform-jomee-jomaa-launched-3444451)). Its terms identify it as a Bangladeshi financial-technology company and explain purchase/POA pathways and document checking ([terms](https://www.jomeejomaa.com/terms)); its site lists RJSC incorporation C-191758/2023 and trade/BIN details ([offerings](https://www.jomeejomaa.com/offerings)). A 2025 IDLC magazine interview contains founder-described tokenization, quarterly revaluation and 80/20 profit split ([IDLC magazine PDF](https://idlc.com/mbr/download/2025/MBRJanuary2025.pdf)).

**JCX use:** model fractional access and digital investor reporting, not an automatic investment partnership. Before any pilot, obtain Bangladesh Bank/BSEC/other counsel opinion on collective investment, securities, AML/KYC, custody, beneficial ownership, secondary-market promises, foreign/NRB participation, tax and Islamic-finance positioning. Do not advertise “20%+ annual ROI” as a forecast; that is a company marketing claim.

#### “BPC” — remove from the active PropTech shortlist unless the user means a different entity

Searches found no credible, property-technology company clearly identified as “BPC Bangladesh.” Results predominantly refer to Bangladesh Petroleum Corporation, Business Promotion Council or Bangladesh Computer Samity. Keep the name in a **negative verification log**, not a company benchmark, until a legal name/domain is supplied. **Grade D / unresolved.**

#### Weak or unverified names: do not silently promote

Searches did not produce adequate dated evidence for a Bangladesh PropTech company named **“NirmanBazaar”** beyond the live company pages (so it is included with a D-grade traction caveat), nor for additional requested “BPC” identity. A live domain is not proof of customers, legal authorization, solvency or title quality. The same standard should be applied to any new pitch deck or LinkedIn-only vendor.

### Bangladesh developers to study as buyers/design partners

JCX should interview major developer archetypes—not assume that a large developer is itself a PropTech company. REHAB’s directory is the best broad membership source. High-value design-partner targets include bti, Sheltech, Eastern Housing, Bashundhara/East West Property Development, Shanta, Concord, Navana, Rangs, Anwar Landmark, Assure, Rupayan and similar multi-project groups. Their relevance is as workflow comparators and potential reference customers. Public websites show live project catalogs and client portals (for example [bti](https://btibd.com/), [Sheltech](https://www.sheltech.com.bd/), [Sheltech Homes portal](https://sheltechhomes.com/)); they do **not**, by themselves, prove internal ERP, AI or smart-building adoption. 

The most concrete adjacent local ecosystem is **smart-home/ELV**, not developer software: SmartLife says it has delivered professional smart-home automation in Bangladesh since 2020, while Credent describes pre-wired IT/ELV, access control, building automation and smart-home-ready infrastructure for developer projects ([SmartLife](https://smartlife.com.bd/about), [Credent real-estate solutions](https://credentbd.com/sectors/real-estate)). These are potential handover/amenity partners; test interoperability, after-sales service, cybersecurity and spare parts.

## 2. India and South Asia

### Sell.Do / Aurum PropTech (India) — benchmark developer revenue OS

Sell.Do was founded in Pune by Ketan Sabnis and Vinayak Katkar; its history describes 2012 founding, 2014 crossing 50+ developer customers, 2015 channel-partner module, 2018 IRIS launch/inventory platform, 2021 Aurum investment and 2024 becoming an Aurum PropTech subsidiary ([About/history](https://www.sell.do/about)). Current product pages claim 1,500+ developers/brokers across 26 cities, US$25bn+ inventory sold, 25m+ leads managed, AI calling/analysis, channel partners, RERA-aware records and possession/collections workflows ([platform](https://www.sell.do/), [developer solution](https://www.sell.do/solutions/developers)). Historical customer counts and outcomes are company claims, but longevity, product depth and public-company backing make it a high-signal benchmark.

Aurum PropTech is listed on NSE/BSE and presents an integrated property ecosystem spanning Sell.Do, housing/rental, analytics and capital ([Aurum PropTech](https://www.aurumproptech.in/), [Aurum about](https://www.aurumproptech.in/about)).

**JCX transfer:** model the full lead→site visit→inventory lock→booking→demand letter→collections→possession chain and channel commissions. Localization: Bengali/Banglish voice, BDT/PDC/mobile money, RAJUK/CDA, JV shares and Bangladesh tax/deed workflows. Risk: India’s RERA process and portal integrations are not Bangladesh legal compliance.

### Landeed (India) — title-search/data workflow

Landeed describes itself as an online property-document search platform; its site references official state records such as EC, Bhoomi RTC, ROR and 7/12, and a reported INR19.5 crore pre-seed round ([About](https://www.landeed.com/about), [product](https://www.landeed.com/)). It is a strong product benchmark for turning fragmented records into a user workflow, but state-by-state Indian data access is not portable to Bangladesh.

**JCX transfer:** build a title-room checklist and evidence graph around khatian, dag, mutation, deed chain, tax receipts, mouza map and encumbrance/litigation review; integrate only through authorized government/partner access. Never present an automated “clean title” score as legal advice.

### NoBroker (India) — scaled consumer marketplace and monetization caution

NoBroker’s own historical press release describes its brokerage-free marketplace, expansion to major Indian cities and early **US$3m Series A** (SAIF/Fulcrum) ([company press PDF](https://assets.nobroker.in/static/img/press/nb_in_chennai.pdf)). Its later annual report remains a useful source for the company’s property-market observations ([2025 report PDF](https://www.nobroker.in/forum/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NoBroker_Realestate_Report_2025_compressed.pdf)). Public reporting and user complaints indicate the practical tension between “no broker” positioning and paid plans/assisted services; treat that as a business-model caution, not a verified finding of misconduct.

**JCX transfer:** owner/developer self-service, rental and society-management UX; avoid dependence on a pure lead marketplace where inventory quality and agent disintermediation are hard to control.

### Infra.Market (India) — construction supply scale

Infra.Market says it was founded in 2016 and is a construction-material company with multi-category manufacturing/distribution; its site provides company information and records a Series Seed allotment ([About](https://infra.market/about-us)). An investor/transaction document described a US$2.5bn valuation (dated 2025 document) ([document](https://infra-prod.gumlet.io/ivas/PROD/06-05-2025/1746534342377_IVAS_DezineMandapam.pdf)); Axios reported a US$50m 2024 raise ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-pro-rata-f336e149-c6af-498b-bf2f-68f2-d73d9d9c6a)) but the link should be treated as secondary evidence. 

**JCX transfer:** combine manufacturer sourcing, private-label quality control, demand aggregation and working-capital/credit data. Bangladesh risks are distributor relationships, port/fuel volatility, quality adulteration, VAT invoices, credit losses and local logistics; start with a non-critical category and compare landed cost rather than copying the balance-sheet-heavy model.

### Powerplay (India) — construction collaboration/site workflow

Accel identifies Powerplay as a seed investment in 2021 and describes it as digitally native management tools for real estate, construction and architecture ([Accel portfolio](https://www.accel.com/companies/powerplay)). Public evidence in this review is stronger for existence/venture backing than for customer outcomes.

**JCX transfer:** daily site diary, drawings/RFI/submittals, work progress, issue/photo evidence and contractor collaboration. Make offline Android, Bangla labels, low-bandwidth sync and role-based contractor access non-negotiable.

### Facilio (India-founded, global) — property operations/IoT

Facilio announced **US$6.4m Series A in December 2018** (Tiger Global and Accel) and **US$35m Series B on 22 February 2022** (Dragoneer, Brookfield Growth, Accel and Tiger), describing an AI/IoT platform for maintenance, operations, sustainability and tenant experience ([Series A](https://facilio.com/newsroom/series-a/), [Series B](https://facilio.com/newsroom/series-b/)). The current company page lists 25,000 buildings future-proofed and 50+ global partners; the 150+ enterprise-client and efficiency statistics on product pages are company claims ([about](https://facilio.in/about/), [contact/metrics](https://facilio.in/contact/)).

**JCX transfer:** post-handover CMMS, preventive maintenance, vendor SLAs, asset register, tenant requests, energy/carbon dashboards. Bangladesh localization is sensor availability, connectivity, maintenance contractor capability and electricity-meter integration—not just translation. Risk: IoT retrofit capex, vendor lock-in, cybersecurity and weak maintenance data discipline.

### Zenatix by Schneider Electric (India) — energy/building automation with strategic acquirer

Zenatix says Schneider Electric now owns/backs the business and reports IoT automation/monitoring deployed across **3,000+ sites**, with typical ROI under 24 months (company claim) ([about](https://www.zenatix.com/about/)). Schneider materials describe Zenatix as an IoT-based energy solution acquired into its building/energy portfolio ([Schneider report](https://download.schneider-electric.com/files?p_Doc_Ref=Verdantix_2023_Report&p_File_Name=Verdantix_Green_Quadrant_Energy_Management_Software_2023_Schneider_Electric.pdf)).

**JCX transfer:** energy monitoring and HVAC/lighting optimization in commercial or premium residential assets; first establish sub-metering and baseline. Solar/backup power, load shedding, device maintenance and data privacy materially change payback in Bangladesh.

## 3. MENA: UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt

### UAE

#### Huspy — brokerage + mortgage workflow

Huspy announced a May 2024 investment round led by Balderton Capital with Fifth Wall and said it operated in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Madrid; its 2022 Series A was **US$37m** led by Peak XV/Sequoia India ([2024 round](https://huspy.com/ae/blog/balderton-huspy), [2022 Series A](https://huspy.com/blog/huspy-series-a)). In a December 2023 company release it claimed AED1bn+ of mortgages processed in November 2023 and more than 25% of Dubai residential mortgages (company claim, not a regulator statistic) ([release](https://www.huspy.com/ae/blog/record-aed-1-billion-in-mortgages-processed-november-2023)).

**JCX transfer:** integrate discover→finance→close, especially for NRBs and first-time buyers. Bangladesh requires lender-specific APIs, Bangladesh Bank foreign-exchange/NRB rules, local credit underwriting, Islamic products and Bengali documentation; do not transplant UAE brokerage licensing assumptions.

#### SmartCrowd and Stake — regulated fractional-investment patterns, not Bangladesh-ready rails

SmartCrowd explains its Dubai structure: each property is held in an SPV, divided into shares and registered/referenced through DIFC/DLD processes; the company says it operates under DFSA supervision ([structure](https://help-center.smartcrowd.ae/en/articles/7122055-how-are-investments-structured-at-smartcrowd)). Stake describes UAE/Saudi investment pathways and explicitly says Bangladesh is currently unavailable to UAE investors ([eligibility](https://help.getstake.com/en/articles/6570336-who-can-invest-in-uae), [onboarding](https://help.getstake.com/en/articles/8249047-how-do-i-begin)). These are legal-structure and eligibility facts from their help centers; returns, liquidity and “first/leading” positioning require separate verification.

**JCX transfer:** transparent investor dashboard, SPV/document vault, distributions and exit reporting. Bangladesh needs securities/collective-investment, land-title, tax, AML/KYC, custody, foreign-exchange and secondary-market counsel before fractionalizing any JCX asset.

#### PRYPCO / Dubai Land Department — tokenization as regulator-led infrastructure

Dubai Land Department reported its second tokenized project on the PRYPCO Mint platform fully funded in **1m58s**, with 149 investors from 35 nationalities, under DLD’s Property Tokenization Initiative ([DLD announcement](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-land-department-achieves-world-first-with-instant-sell-out-of-tokenized-real-estate)). PRYPCO describes itself as a platform for investing, financing and owning property ([help page](https://help.prypco.com/en/articles/13740985-what-is-prypco)).

**Lesson:** tokenization scales when land registry, regulator, SPV and investor identity are connected. A blockchain wrapper without government recognition, enforceable beneficial ownership and a real exit is marketing, not infrastructure.

#### Facilio’s UAE footprint

Facilio lists Dubai offices and Dubai portfolio examples, including ICD/Brookfield Place, and names UAE operators among customers/partners ([commercial portfolio](https://facilio.in/industry/commercial-portfolios/), [contact](https://facilio.in/contact/)). This is a practical benchmark for premium mixed-use operations; the Dubai implementation is not proof of Bangladesh building-system compatibility.

### Saudi Arabia

#### BRKZ — procurement + embedded finance

BRKZ launched in 2022 and announced a **US$8m Series A in March 2024** co-led by 9900 and BECO Capital, bringing total reported funding to US$13.55m; the model connects contractors and suppliers, with delivery/payment options and plans for equipment/manpower ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2024/03/brkz-secures-8-million-series-round), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/03/05/brkz-emerges-from-stealth-with-8m/)). BRKZ’s February 2025 release reported a US$17m Series A total and US$22.5m total funding, including an extension and venture debt ([release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/02/10/3023492/0/en/BRKZ-closes-17M-Series-A-to-transform-construction-procurement-as-Saudi-Arabia-s-3T-project-pipeline-accelerates.html)); it is a company release, so validate financial statements and current operations.

**JCX transfer:** RFQ aggregation, supplier scoring, predictable delivery and construction-cycle credit. Bangladesh needs local mill/dealer onboarding, quality certificates, transport routing, VAT and credit-loss controls; avoid financing inventory before demand/collections data are reliable.

#### WakeCap — connected site visibility and acquisitions/partnerships

WakeCap describes a Saudi-based smart construction project-management platform using smart hard hats for worker activity/equipment insights; in April 2024 it announced a partnership with OpenSpace for local access to reality capture/AI analytics ([partnership](https://www.wakecap.com/news-and-events/wakecap-openspace-partnership)). In July 2024 it announced acquisition of Crews by Core and said it had projects in the US, Saudi Arabia, UAE and Japan ([acquisition](https://www.wakecap.com/news-and-events/wakecap-acquires-crews-by-core)).

**JCX transfer:** progressive site evidence, safety and productivity; pair with Powerplay-style document collaboration. Bangladesh labor/privacy norms, device affordability, heat/monsoon/offline operation and worker consent must be designed in.

#### Saudi PropTech Hub / REGA — ecosystem and regulatory route

REGA launched the Saudi PropTech Hub in October 2024 with a regulatory sandbox, accelerator and ecosystem programs, aligned with Vision 2030 ([REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/platforms/saudi-proptech-hub/), [Saudi Press Agency](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2196077)). **JCX lesson:** Bangladesh needs a similar developer–regulator sandbox for e-registration/e-mutation integrations, title APIs, digital signatures, fractional investment and building/energy data. Partnerships with Ministry of Land, RAJUK, banks, REHAB, universities and Startup Bangladesh are more valuable than isolated pilots.

## Cross-region patterns and what not to copy

1. **Workflow before marketplace.** Sell.Do, PropERP, Vextrus and Facilio succeed conceptually because they sit inside daily operations. A portal is easy to launch and hard to defend.
2. **Data rights are the moat.** Landeed, GA/RENOSY, 99 Group and Archistar turn records/transactions/planning rules into reusable intelligence. JCX should own clean, consented project, lead, unit, payment, procurement and outcome data with an exportable schema.
3. **Finance follows evidence.** BRKZ and Infra.Market can offer credit because procurement and repayment data are embedded; Huspy/Nawy connect transaction and mortgage rails. Do not add BNPL/fractional investment before title, KYC, collection and default data are robust.
4. **Regulator-connected models scale trust.** Dubai DLD/PRYPCO and Saudi REGA sandbox show that tokenization/innovation needs a registry/regulator route. Bangladesh e-Mutation/e-registration integration should be pursued institutionally.
5. **AI must draft, not silently decide.** Local language and document variability make human approval, confidence, provenance, audit logs and rollback essential. Avoid “AI valuation,” “clean title,” “zero disputes,” “zero double booking” or “20% ROI” as unqualified marketing.
6. **Physical operations remain decisive.** Construction marketplaces fail if quality, delivery, credit and returns are not controlled; smart-building systems fail if sensors are not maintained; portals fail if listings are stale. Pilot operational KPIs, not feature counts.

## JCX whitespace and recommended sequencing

### Highest-priority whitespace

**A. Bangladesh developer OS.** One canonical project/unit/customer/landowner record; land/JV allocation; booking and installment schedules; broker commissions; construction/procurement; collections/reconciliation; handover/registry; bilingual customer portal. This is the most defensible first wedge because it creates recurring operational data.

**B. Title and feasibility evidence graph.** Store each claim with source document, date, reviewer, geometry and confidence. Pull authorized government records where possible; use Barikoi/geospatial layers and Archistar-like feasibility calculations as decision support, never a title guarantee.

**C. Procurement control tower.** RFQ → approved vendor → PO → delivery/GRN → invoice/VAT → payment, with local price history and quality certificates. Benchmark BuildMart/NirmanBazaar/BRKZ/Infra.Market; keep financing separate until default risk is understood.

**D. Trust layer for JV/shareholders.** ShareBari/Truzo patterns—private statements, proof-linked expenses, committee approvals, progress evidence—can materially reduce disputes in Bangladesh’s shared apartment model.

**E. Post-handover operating and energy layer.** Facilio/Zenatix patterns for ticketing, preventive maintenance, asset registers, submetering and carbon. Start with premium/mixed-use assets where service charges and energy savings can pay for adoption.

### 0–90 days: instrument one JCX project

Baseline booking-to-collection time; lead response and conversion; double-booking incidents; approval cycle; procurement quote spread and delivery variance; rework/wastage; site-report completeness; customer complaints; handover defects; electricity per sqm. Select one project and one workflow owner. Require APIs/export, role-based access, bilingual labels, offline mobile, immutable audit history, daily backups and incident response.

### 3–9 months: integrate, don’t replace everything

Pilot PropERP/Vextrus/E-Hishabi for developer workflows; BuildMart/NirmanBazaar for a bounded procurement category; Barikoi for geospatial identity; ShareBari/Truzo for a shared/JV project; Powerplay/WakeCap-like site evidence; Facilio/Zenatix-like operations only where instrumentation exists. Compare vendor TCO and adoption against a controlled baseline.

### 9–24 months: build JCX data products

Create a governed internal “JCX property graph”; expose customer-facing progress/payment transparency; publish methodology-backed project and location insights; add partner APIs for banks, legal/title providers and maps; evaluate fractional/alternative financing only after counsel and audited controls. A future public website can visualize this landscape and JCX’s own evidence without turning marketing claims into facts.

## Diligence checklist for every vendor

- Legal entity, beneficial ownership, incorporation, licenses and current status; distinguish acquired/rebranded/domain-only assets.
- Three named customers who permit reference calls; live workflow demo on JCX-like data; independent outcome evidence.
- Data location, ownership, export/deletion, retention, sub-processors, breach process and cross-border transfer.
- Security: MFA, RBAC, encryption, audit logs, backups, RPO/RTO, penetration testing, segregation of tenant data.
- Integration: API/webhooks, accounting/ERP, SMS/WhatsApp, bKash/Nagad/banks, GIS, document OCR, CSV fallback.
- Local fit: Bengali/Banglish, BDT/lakh/crore, fiscal year, VAT/Mushak, TDS/VDS, PDC, khatian/dag/mutation, RAJUK/CDA/DAP, JV ratios and NRB process.
- Commercial reality: implementation owner, support SLA, uptime, price escalators, per-user/per-project limits, lock-in and exit.
- Risk controls: title/legal responsibility, AI human approval, procurement quality/returns, credit losses, fractional-investment regulation, accessibility and worker/customer consent.
