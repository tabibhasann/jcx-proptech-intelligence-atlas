## Honest remaining limitations

- China is now represented by a material public comparator, but this is not a Mandarin-language census of the Chinese market.
- English-accessible US and specialist venture ecosystems remain disproportionately observable.
- Country-specific planning, title, payments, consumer protection, data governance and implementation capacity require local diligence.
- Company, vendor, customer and ecosystem claims remain attributed; inclusion does not establish causality, procurement fitness, security, financial health or realized JCX value.
- The 44-profile launch selection is a narrative sample. The 295 qualified records and 978-identity discovery frontier remain available for deeper exploration.
