# JCX enterprise-platform coverage audit

> **Historical pre-integration audit:** its 16 recommended enterprise/platform additions are now integrated. Use `research/final_release_validation.md` and `data/atlas_manifest.json` for current release facts; retain this file for selection rationale.

**Audit date:** 30 August 2026 (Asia/Dhaka)  
**Scope:** enterprise platforms and high-impact built-environment scale-ups that a developer, owner, operator or institutional investor would buy, procure or integrate. Covered domains are development/investment management; construction CDE and payment; property ERP; leasing/CRM; BMS/building controls; and climate/property risk.  
**Inputs checked:** `data/atlas_entities.csv` (228 records), `research/global_real_estate_pioneers_agent.csv` (77 records), discovery/source registers and the existing research narrative. No existing corpus, generator or atlas files were edited.

## Executive verdict

The qualified core already has a credible base in Yardi, MRI Software, AppFolio, Entrata, Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud / Forma, Trimble, Bentley Systems, Hexagon, Nemetschek, Measurabl, Deepki, Kestrix, Facilio, BrainBox AI, SmartRent, VTS, LandTech, Orbital Witness, HouseCanary, Cherre, TestFit, Buildots, Built Technologies, Rabbet, Snapdocs and Salesforce Real Estate Cloud. The discovery layer also records some of the candidates below, but discovery-only presence is not a qualified profile.

The material gap is not another long vendor list. It is the missing enterprise control plane around the asset lifecycle:

1. **Development and investment truth:** Northspyre, Dealpath, Juniper Square and Altus ARGUS cover development approvals, acquisition workflow, fund operations and underwriting/valuation. Without them, JCX overweights portals and point solutions and under-represents the systems that control capital decisions.
2. **Construction execution and payment:** Oracle Construction & Engineering (Aconex, Textura, Primavera Cloud and Unifier) is a major CDE/workflow/payment suite absent from the qualified core. It is a materially different enterprise incumbent from Procore/Autodesk and a relevant procurement/integration benchmark.
3. **Residential operating systems:** RealPage and EliseAI fill the missing multifamily ERP and AI leasing/resident workflow layers. RealPage is also a useful incumbent-scale counterpoint to AppFolio and Entrata; EliseAI is a high-impact AI scale-up rather than a generic chatbot.
4. **Controls incumbency and interoperability:** Siemens Building X, Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Building, Johnson Controls OpenBlue and Honeywell Forge for Buildings are the global BMS/OT control-plane comparators. They matter for retrofit, energy, cyber, device interoperability and owner/operator data rights.
5. **Climate and property-risk decisioning:** Climate X, First Street and ZestyAI show three distinct decision surfaces: institutional asset-level financial risk, public/building-level physical-risk data, and insurer-grade property risk. The current core has climate/energy platforms but not this complete risk-to-capital bridge.

The companion `enterprise_platform_additions.csv` contains **16 additions**. Tier 1 is an anchor profile; Tier 2 is a comparative/context profile where geography, maturity or evidence is narrower. Company-reported footprint, funding, AUM, customer and outcome figures remain attributed claims, not independently assured ROI.

## Current-core comparison

Exact-name and normalized parent/product checks found **no qualified atlas entity** for Oracle Construction & Engineering/Aconex/Textura, RealPage, Northspyre, Juniper Square, Dealpath, EliseAI, Siemens Building X, Schneider EcoStruxure Building, Johnson Controls OpenBlue, Honeywell Forge, Runwise, PassiveLogic, Climate X, First Street, ZestyAI or Altus ARGUS. Juniper Square, Dealpath, EliseAI, Runwise and PassiveLogic are present in discovery-only ecosystem records; that is useful lead provenance, not coverage. Oracle Textura appears in a source inventory/narrative URL but has no corresponding qualified entity.

Existing rows are not duplicated here: Procore and Autodesk cover CDE/construction; Yardi, MRI, AppFolio and Entrata cover property ERP; Measurabl, Deepki, Kestrix, Facilio, BrainBox AI and SmartRent cover adjacent energy/building workflows; VTS covers leasing/marketing. The recommendations below add missing enterprise control planes or decision-critical scale, rather than adjacent features.

## Thin or collision-prone areas (do not add more rows yet)

- **Existing construction platforms:** Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud / Forma, Trimble and Bentley are present, but the current qualified rows should extract named CDE, cost, field, model, payment and handover modules before adding more point tools. Oracle is the missing suite-level incumbent.
- **Existing property ERPs:** Yardi, MRI Software, AppFolio and Entrata are present. RealPage should be added as the missing multifamily incumbent; do not add separate rows for every RealPage AI/property module.
- **Existing energy/building platforms:** Measurabl, Deepki, Kestrix, Facilio, BrainBox AI and SmartRent are present. The four OEM platforms above are not redundant: their installed-base controls, OT protocols, cybersecurity and service channels are the strategic distinction.
- **Discovery-only records:** Juniper Square, Dealpath, EliseAI, Runwise and PassiveLogic have ecosystem/discovery entries but no qualified atlas entity. Upgrade only the canonical platform row; do not treat an investor portfolio card as traction evidence.
- **Product/parent collisions:** Use Oracle Construction & Engineering as the entity scope for Aconex/Textura/Primavera/Unifier; Siemens Building X, EcoStruxure Building, OpenBlue and Honeywell Forge for Buildings as product/platform scopes rather than generic parent-company rows; use Altus Group ARGUS as one family rather than ARGUS module duplicates.
- **Low-value exclusions:** Do not add general-purpose Salesforce/AI/cloud vendors beyond the existing Salesforce Real Estate Cloud row, or generic engineering/ERP suites without a built-environment product, current deployment evidence and a clear JCX decision implication.

## Scope limits and confidence

This is an omission audit against the current structured atlas and qualified-core intent, not a global vendor census. It is strongest for English-language, publicly documented enterprise platforms and large North American/European/global OEMs. China/domestic APAC platforms, state-owned systems, private regional ERPs and non-English vendors need a dedicated regional pass. Runwise, PassiveLogic, First Street and ZestyAI have narrower geography or maturity than the Tier 1 incumbents.

**High confidence:** Oracle Construction & Engineering, RealPage, Juniper Square, Dealpath, Altus ARGUS, Siemens Building X, Schneider EcoStruxure Building, Johnson Controls OpenBlue, Honeywell Forge and Climate X are material omissions with current first-party evidence.  
**Medium-high confidence:** Northspyre and EliseAI are high-impact scale-ups with strong product evidence but more company-reported traction; Runwise is decision-relevant for retrofit portfolios; First Street and ZestyAI are valuable risk comparators but geography/model-specific.  
**Medium confidence:** PassiveLogic is strategically important as a frontier autonomy case, but public deployment and outcome evidence is not yet sufficient for Tier 1.

Before publication, resolve legal entity/product relationships, capture current status dates, preserve source-level evidence grades and separate vendor claims from independently assured customer outcomes. The recommended rows are intended to make JCX’s build/buy/integrate decisions legible—not to imply that any platform is transferable to Bangladesh without local data, protocol, procurement, payment, cybersecurity and regulatory diligence.
