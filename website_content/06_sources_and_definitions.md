## Metric definitions

| ID | Term | Definition for this package | Common mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Portal | Search and advertising marketplace where professionals pay for visibility, leads or data | Treating portal revenue as commission revenue |
| M02 | Brokerage | Human and software workflow that helps a transaction close and collects a fee | Treating an enquiry or listing as a completed deal |
| M03 | iBuying | Company buys, holds and resells the home on its own balance sheet | Treating resale revenue as a light marketplace fee |
| M04 | Managed marketplace | Platform combines discovery with operational services such as documents, visits, payment or protection | Ignoring the people and claims process behind the interface |
| M05 | Revenue | Amount recognized by the company under its accounting definition | Confusing it with gross transaction value |
| M06 | Operating profit / net income | Operating profit is before financing and some non-operating items; net income is after the full accounting result | Mixing statutory operating profit, adjusted EBITDA and net income |
| M07 | GTV | Gross value of properties or contracts transacted through a platform | Treating the full property value as company revenue |
| M08 | Take rate / commission | Fee divided by the transaction value, with the payer and collection rule stated | Ignoring affiliate splits, unpaid fees or different denominators |
| M09 | Asking price | Price requested in a listing | Calling it a closing price |
| M10 | Registered/document value | Value recorded in a deed, registry or document | Assuming it equals negotiated consideration |
| M11 | Closing price | Price actually paid at completion, with date, property and verification | Building an index from unverified asking prices |
| M12 | Pilot | Limited test or experiment | Calling a pilot deployment or ROI |
| M13 | Adoption | Eligible users or teams actually using a system, with a denominator and period | Replacing adoption with licenses sold, a survey pilot or pilots announced |
| M14 | Operating result | Measured change in time, errors, service volume, cash, retention or another stated outcome | Assigning portfolio growth to one tool without causal evidence |

## Editorial rules

- Preserve currency, period, geography and denominator.
- Put the source beside every material visible number.
- Mark company-reported outcomes as company-reported.
- Use "the public evidence reviewed here" instead of absolute claims about all of Bangladesh.
- Never present the private client forecast, internal budget, private risk board or unapproved strategy on the public site.
